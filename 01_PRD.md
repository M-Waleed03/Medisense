# MEDISENSE — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## App Name
MEDISENSE

## Tagline
AI-powered healthcare assistant for symptom analysis, medical report interpretation, and smart health guidance.

## Problem Statement
Many people struggle to understand symptoms and medical reports such as CBC, dengue, malaria, and typhoid tests. Lack of medical knowledge often causes self-medication, delayed treatment, and misdiagnosis. In rural and underserved areas, access to doctors and healthcare facilities is limited.

MEDISENSE solves this problem by providing an AI-driven healthcare platform that analyzes symptoms and medical reports, predicts possible illnesses, and provides safe recommendations and next-step guidance.

## Target Users
### Primary Users
- General public seeking quick healthcare guidance
- People living in rural or underserved regions
- Students and working professionals
- Families monitoring health conditions at home

### Secondary Users
- Clinics and healthcare providers
- Diagnostic laboratories
- Future telemedicine integrations

## Core Value Proposition
- AI symptom checking
- OCR-based report analysis
- Disease prediction
- AI chatbot guidance
- Health history tracking

## Must-Have Features
- User authentication
- AI symptom checker
- OCR-based report analysis
- AI chatbot
- Dashboard with history
- Mobile + web support

## Nice-to-Have Features
- Voice assistant
- Wearable integration
- Multi-language support
- Telemedicine support

## Out of Scope
- Replacing licensed doctors
- Emergency medical treatment
- Prescription medicine delivery

## User Stories
- As a user, I want to enter symptoms so I can know possible diseases.
- As a user, I want to upload reports so I can understand results.
- As a user, I want AI recommendations for next-step guidance.

## Success Metrics
- 1,000+ users
- Fast OCR processing
- High chatbot engagement
- Secure authentication
